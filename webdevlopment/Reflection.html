My Football Journey

My Goals and Growth

Football has always been more than just a game to me. It has given me opportunities that I probably would never have gotten otherwise, and it has taught me a lot about myself. Through football, I have learned the importance of hard work, dedication, and staying focused on my goals. From playing at Clear Springs to going to Houston Christian and now being at Missouri, every step of my journey has been different. I have experienced some great moments, but I have also been through some of the toughest times in my life. Looking back, I can see how every experience has helped me grow, not only as a football player but also as a person.

My time at Houston Christian was a very important part of my football career. Having a good season there opened up many different opportunities and showed me that I was capable of taking the next step. That experience helped me realize that I did not want to stop there. I wanted to keep improving and find a new challenge that would push me even harder. Football has taught me that success does not mean you have reached the end of your journey. There is always something new to learn, another level to reach, and another way to improve. My time at Houston Christian helped prepare me for the next chapter of my career and gave me the motivation to keep moving forward.

Now that I am at Missouri, I have the opportunity to challenge myself in a new environment and compete at the SEC level. This is something I am excited about because I know that being surrounded by talented players and competing against strong teams will push me to become better. I understand that this next step will come with challenges, and I know that I will have to continue working hard both on and off the field. I want to take advantage of every opportunity I get, learn from the people around me, and continue developing my skills. Being at Missouri is another important step in my journey, and I am ready to see how much I can grow during this experience.

My biggest goal is to play in the NFL, and I know that reaching that level will take a lot of hard work, patience, and commitment. It is not something that will happen overnight, and there will be challenges along the way. I have to stay focused, keep working toward my goals, and remember what I am trying to accomplish no matter what happens. I know that there will be good days and difficult days, but I want to keep pushing forward and make the most of every opportunity. This website is a way for me to share where I have been, where I am now, and where I want to go in the future. It represents my journey so far and my commitment to continuing to grow as a player while working toward my dream of playing in the NFL.

Xaishaun Edwards, 9/9/2026
